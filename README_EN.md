# 🦞 Job Seeker - Career Advisor AI Assistant

## Introduction

The "Career Advisor" is an AI assistant designed to help job seekers find their ideal jobs:
- 🔍 Search job listings from major recruitment platforms
- 📊 Analyze company backgrounds and reputations
- 🎯 Recommend suitable positions based on your needs

---

## Getting Started

### Basic Conversation

Just message me directly and tell me about yourself:

```
Hi, I'm looking for a job
```

Or describe your needs:

```
Help me find algorithm engineer positions in Shanghai
```

---

## First Use - Information Collection

On first use, I'll ask you a few questions to understand your profile:

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Fresh graduate or experienced? | Determines which platforms to search |
| Education and major? | Matches job requirements |
| Which city/cities? | Location filtering |
| What position? | Job direction |

---

## Position Details

Tell me which position interests you, and I'll provide in-depth analysis:

**Analysis includes:**
- Job match rate (JD requirements vs your skills)
- Salary competitiveness
- Company background and reputation
- Potential risk warnings

---

## Feedback & Optimization

After each recommendation, please share your thoughts:

| Your Feedback | Assistant Action |
|--------------|------------------|
| "Too tired" | Emphasize work-life-balance analysis |
| "Salary too low" | Focus on salary competitiveness |
| "Want stability" | Recommend state-owned enterprises |
| "Want growth" | Recommend top companies/core business |

---

## Search Scope

### Recruitment Platforms
- Boss Zhipin, Niuke, Lagou (Internet)
- Zhaopin, 51Job (Traditional)
- Liepin (Senior positions)
- Guopin (State-owned enterprises)

### Official Channels
- University career websites (campus recruit)
- Government talent programs (public sector)
- Company official career pages

### Reputation Research
- Xiaohongshu (employee experience)
- Maimai (workplace reviews)
- Zhihu (company analysis)

---

## Limitations

- ⚠️ Does not apply to jobs, only provides information
- ⚠️ Salary data from the internet, for reference only
- ⚠️ Make decisions carefully, verify from multiple sources
