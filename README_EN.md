# clawskills-all-in-one

Save, update and share OpenClaw Skills tools.

---

## 📦 Skills

### job-seeker

**Function**: AI assistant that helps job seekers search recruitment platforms, analyze company reputations, and recommend suitable positions.

**Install**: Just tell me "Install job-seeker skill (https://github.com/cszr-liuxiaobo/clawskills-all-in-one/tree/main/skills/job-seeker)", and I will automatically read SKILL.md and complete the installation.

Or manually: Add `"job-seeker": { "enabled": true }` to `skills.entries` in `openclaw.json`, then restart OpenClaw.

**Details**: [SKILL_EN.md](./skills/job-seeker/SKILL_EN.md)
